Our Story

A taste of Yemen,
plated with heart

At BISMI MANDI, mandi is more than a dish — it is a ritual. Long-grain rice is steeped in fragrant spices and smoked slow over gentle heat, until every grain carries the aroma of the embers. Atop it rests meat so tender it gives way at the touch of a spoon.

We cook the way it has always been done — patiently, generously, and with the conviction that every guest should leave feeling cared for. That is the spirit of asli Yemeni mandi.

  • Traditional smoked dum, never shortcuts
  • Fresh, halal cuts prepared daily
  • Generous portions made for sharing
